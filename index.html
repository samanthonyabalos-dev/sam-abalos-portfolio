02 / About me

What I actually
work on.

My background includes UGC ads, YouTube documentaries, short-form clipping, and content management. I mainly edit in CapCut, with Canva and AI tools when the project calls for them.

I'm used to working from a client's brief and references, getting footage into shape, and making changes after review.

01

UGC & short-form

Picking the best takes, removing filler and repeated lines, matching B-roll to voiceovers, and keeping cuts natural. Some projects start with 15–20 minutes of raw footage and end at around a minute.

02

Long-form YouTube

Building narrated visual stories from supplied scripts and audio, sourcing footage, and managing the pace across longer edits.

03

Creative support

Color grading, captions, Canva graphics, AI-assisted visuals, content organization, and revisions.
